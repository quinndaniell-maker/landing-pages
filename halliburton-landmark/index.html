The pattern behind the acquisition

Halliburton's product organizations are deliberately kept siloed — deep domain expertise, product by product. It's a strength. It's also a known, admitted tradeoff.

"Our organization is structured in a way that we deliberately chose to be siloed — individual product lines with deep domain expertise... we call it organizational friction." — a Halliburton product strategy leader

Every product team ends up building its own roadmap format, its own status deck, its own way of tracking dependencies. That's manageable at one product's scale. It compounds fast once a portfolio spans as many lines as Landmark's now does, especially with new product lines just joining the mix.
